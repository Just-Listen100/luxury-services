01

The smartest homes leak the most

A modern automated home is a fleet of small computers, and almost every one is designed to send your life somewhere else. Cameras streaming to a company's cloud. Locks whose codes live on other people's servers. Assistants that record first and ask later. Thermostats, doorbells, and TVs reporting patterns a stranger can read.

For most people that is an inconvenience. For an executive, an investor, or a public figure, that pattern is a target: when you are home, when you travel, who visits, where the safe is. The more connected the house, the bigger the surface an attacker gets for free.

03

Three promises no cloud vendor can make

Nothing calls home

There is no backend to breach because there is no backend. No vendor holds your footage, your codes, or your routines, because no vendor ever receives them. There is simply no outside system to attack.

Offline by default

Cut the internet line and nothing stops working. The system treats connectivity as optional, never required, which means the most common path into a modern home, its internet connection, leads to nothing worth stealing.

Encrypted end to end

Everything the system records or stores is encrypted by default, end to end. The only keys that can read your home's data are inside your home. Not ours. Yours.

06

Questions you should ask us

What happens if the internet goes out?

Nothing changes inside your house. The system is designed to run fully offline; connectivity is treated as an optional convenience, never a dependency.

Who can see my cameras, logs, or automation data?

Only you, from inside your own home. The data never leaves your property, and it is encrypted end to end against anyone who tried to take it.

Do you offer monitoring?

No, and that is the point. A monitoring service means a stranger's eyes on your home and a data trail leaving it. We build systems strong enough not to need that, and private enough not to allow it.

What does it cost?

Every property is different, so every build is quoted on its own design after the consultation. You pay for the system and you own it. There is no recurring fee, because nothing keeps running on our side after handover.

Is this compatible with my existing smart devices?

Much of it, yes, once those devices are brought inside a hardened, private network instead of phoning their manufacturer's cloud. Some cloud-dependent devices are replaced; the consultation tells you exactly which and why.
